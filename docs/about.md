## I am not trying to fool the detectors

I want to say this plainly, because it is the first thing anyone assumes about a tool like
this.

I cannot play instruments. Not keys, not guitar, and realistically I never will. But I like
generating music, and sometimes I do not like how certain parts of it sound. My suspicion is
that whatever bothers my ear is exactly the "AI-ness" the detectors are reacting to.

That is what got me interested in analysis: first find *where* the problem sits, then find
fixes for it — small scripted flows that clean things up — so that I am not publishing audio
that is plainly low quality. All of this is about studying AI-generated sound and improving
it. It is not about hiding anything from anyone.

So when a detector tells me a track reads as generated, I treat that as useful information
about my mix. It is not a score I am trying to beat.

## I am not a musician

I am not a musician. Not a singer, not a poet, not a creator, not a talent. I do have a sense
of rhythm, and I stomp along very well — that is roughly where my credentials end. I am a
programmer, although at this point I am not entirely sure I am even that anymore.

I am not competing with anyone, for anything, and I am not claiming anything. I mess around in
Suno making silly derivative little songs, trying to make something that I myself enjoy. It
breaks up the monotony of being alive. That is the entire ambition.

If any of it turns out to be useful to you as well — good. That is a bonus, not the plan.
